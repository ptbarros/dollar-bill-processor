Which patterns actually earn their keep?

Your full scan history — every bill the app has flagged, cross-referenced against the ones you cropped to keep. The gap between how often a pattern fires and how often you keep it is the whole story.

How to read this / caveats

  • "Kept" = cropped. Cropping a bill is your judgment that it's worth saving to possibly sell — a strong proxy for value, but not a sale.
  • Rates below 8 hits are omitted from the ranked lists — a 1-for-1 pattern reads as "100% kept" on a single bill and would mislead. All patterns still appear on the map.
  • Aliases were merged by identical hit-sets: patterns firing on the exact same bills are shown as one group — the live duplicate-pattern signal. Only groups with ≥8 shared hits are treated as confirmed.
  • Generated from your local bill ledger (ledger.db). Re-open this report any time from Tools → Insights; it always reflects the latest data.
